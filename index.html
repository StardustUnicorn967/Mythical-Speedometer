Saved trips

Trips are saved on this device only. Clearing browser website data may delete them. GPS can pause when iPhone locks; distance is an estimate.

GPS speed and altitude come from your iPhone. When temperature is enabled, approximate coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to obtain nearby outdoor weather; no trip history is sent. For the best GPS signal, test outdoors while stationary first. Use your vehicle's own speedometer for driving decisions. Altitude may be unavailable or inaccurate and is not for aviation. Weather needs internet and may differ from roadside conditions. This web app may stop updating when iOS suspends it.
